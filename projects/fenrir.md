# FenRIR — French Robotics Cup 2026

**Collaborative robotics project | RIR — Robotique des Ingénieurs de Roanne, Polytech Lyon**

FenRIR is the primary autonomous competition robot created collectively by the **RIR association and its members** for the 2026 French Robotics Cup. The themed robot manipulates wooden game elements with an electromechanical elevator and gripping mechanisms, alongside smaller Ratatosk PAMIs.

## System overview
The association's published project documentation describes an ESP32-based controller, LiDAR, 12 V power system, stepper-based motion and elevation, suction grippers, servos and auxiliary mechanisms.
