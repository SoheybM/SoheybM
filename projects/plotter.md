# 2D Pen Plotter — Electromechanical Project

**Polytech Lyon | 2024–2025**

## Overview
An academic team developed a belt-driven two-axis drawing system, combining rails, motors, 3D-printed parts, a frame and pen-lift mechanics. The project lead confirms that the prototype successfully produced **automatic drawings**.

## My role
I served as **project lead**, supporting task coordination and project scheduling with GanttProject. Additional hands-on responsibilities will be documented only after confirmation.
